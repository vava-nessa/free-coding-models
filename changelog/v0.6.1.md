# Changelog v0.6.1 - 2026-11-03

Full provider audit: all 25 tracked catalogs checked against their live APIs and official docs, one researcher per provider. 38 model rows removed, 39 added, 8 corrected. Net catalog: **258 models across 24 providers** (onomeo exits the catalog entirely).

### Removed

- **onomeo (whole provider, 15 models)**: the provider removed its ENTIRE free-model lineup (8 tracked models appear in its own "This week's changes" list, the rest were already delisted). The current onomeo catalog is 100% paid, and the only no-charge access left is browser-chat rewards that do not work via API. The provider is retired from the catalog following the github-models/zai pattern: `ONOMEO_API_KEY` config support stays for backwards compatibility, but no onomeo rows are exposed anymore.
- **OpenRouter**: `poolside/laguna-xs-2.1:free` and `poolside/laguna-s-2.1:free` are fully delisted (zero poolside entries left in the live catalog, free or paid) and `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free` lost its free variant (the paid model remains). Replacement on the free shelf: `recursive/flint-v1.0:free`.
- **Kilo**: the same Poolside Laguna pair plus `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free` were removed from the gateway, mirroring OpenRouter's delisting.
- **OpenCode Zen**: 4 free models died since the last audit: `nemotron-3-ultra-free`, `space-bunny-free`, `longcat-2.5-preview-free`, `exo-free` (absent from the live `/v1/models`, completion probes return the same upstream-unavailable error as a bogus id; the docs table is stale on nemotron-3-ultra-free). `jev-1.13-free` stays live even though it is hidden from the public listing.
- **Google AI**: `gemini-3.7-flash` and `gemini-3.5-flash` were deprecated on 2026-10-08 and now auto-route to 3.8/3.6 Flash, so they were duplicate entries and are removed. The gemini-2.5 trio (`2.5-flash`, `2.5-flash-lite`, `2.5-pro`) was deprecated on 2026-11-02 and closes for new users, but still serves existing keys until 2026-12-01: they are kept with a `deprecatedAfter: 2026-12-01` sunset so the catalog auto-filters them on Dec 1 instead of breaking them today.
- **Scaleway**: `glm-5.2` is deprecated (EOL 2026-11-16, requests already route to glm-5.3) and `qwen3-coder-30b-a3b-instruct` lost its Serverless access again (EOL 2026-10-01 re-confirmed, reversing the previous re-add).
- **LLM7**: `kimi-k2.6` and `kimi-k2.7-code` flipped to paid-only (tier=pro, usage_based_only=true; a free token now gets 401). Kimi K2.6 remains free on NVIDIA NIM, Pollinations and Ollama Cloud.
- **Routeway**: the `deepseek-v4-flash:free` and `minimax-m2.7:free` slots rotated to the newer generations (see Added).
- **Mistral LP**: `mistral-medium-3-5` was officially deprecated on 2026-10-31 (retirement 2027-04-30, designated replacement Mistral Large 4), and `mistral-vibe-cli-latest` now resolves to that same deprecated Medium line, so both are removed until Mistral repoints the alias.
- **Vercel AI Gateway**: `poolside/laguna-s-2.1-free` is delisted (only the paid laguna-s-2.1 remains) and the `stealth/cat-jam` stealth slot rotated out.
- **NVIDIA NIM**: `z-ai/glm-5.3` reached end of life on 2026-10-28 (API returns HTTP 410 Gone); `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` remains.
